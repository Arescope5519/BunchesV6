Get Melibri

Melibri collects the recipes you actually cook - saved from the web, scanned out of a cookbook, or written from scratch - and keeps them organised, tagged and ready to cook from.

Melibri is currently in private testing with a small group. The app will appear here on Google Play and the App Store when it launches - check back soon, or hold on to that printed recipe.
