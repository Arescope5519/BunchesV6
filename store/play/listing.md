# Play Store listing - Melibri

Draft copy. Paste into Play Console -> Main store listing.

## App name (30 char max)

```
Melibri
```

## Short description (80 char max)

```
Save, scan and cook the recipes you actually use. No ads, no tracking.
```

69 characters. This is the line shown in search results, so it leads with
what the app does and closes on the thing that genuinely sets it apart.

## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Melibri keeps the recipes you actually cook in one place - saved from the
web, scanned out of a cookbook, or written from scratch.

SAVE FROM ANYWHERE
Share a recipe from your browser and Melibri pulls out the ingredients,
steps and times automatically. No more scrolling past someone's holiday
story to find the oven temperature.

SCAN A COOKBOOK PAGE
Photograph a printed recipe and Melibri reads it into a proper recipe
card you can search, tag and cook from. Handy for the family recipes
that only exist on an index card.

WRITE YOUR OWN
Type in the ones that live in your head. Add a photo whenever you get
round to it.

COOK MODE
A cooking view that works with your hands busy: cross off ingredients
and steps as you go, hide what you have finished, and the screen stays
awake until you are done.

FIND IT AGAIN
Cookbooks, tags and search across every recipe you have saved. The tags
you search most drift to the top of the list on their own.

DIETARY LABELS
Tell Melibri what you avoid and it flags recipes that clash, highlighting
the ingredient responsible. Automated labelling is a guide, not a medical
tool - always check ingredients yourself if you have an allergy.

SHARE WITH FRIENDS
Send a recipe to a friend, or share it as an image. Recipes you keep
private stay private.

NO ADS, NO TRACKING
There is no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking in
Melibri. Your recipes are yours. You can export everything as a backup,
and delete your account from Settings whenever you want.

Melibri is in early access and improving quickly. Tell us what is missing:
hello@melibri.app
```

## Assets in this folder

- `icon-512.png` - 512x512, required
- `feature-graphic.png` - 1024x500, required

Both are generated by `assets/generate-icons.py` from the same mark as
the app icon, so the launcher, the website and the store listing stay in
step. Re-run that script after any change to the mark.

## Screenshots - still needed

Play requires at least 2 phone screenshots, portrait. Worth capturing:

1. Recipes grid with several real recipes and photos
2. A recipe open, showing tags and times
3. Cook Mode partway through, some items crossed off
4. The dietary warning on a recipe that clashes with a preference

Use a device with a clean status bar and recipes that look real - a grid
full of "test test" undersells the app more than anything else in the
listing.

## Notes

- Do not claim the app "identifies allergens". The Terms are explicit
  that labels are keyword-derived guesses, and the store listing must not
  promise more than the app delivers.
- Keep "No ads, no tracking" accurate. If an analytics SDK is ever added,
  this listing and the Privacy Policy both become false.
